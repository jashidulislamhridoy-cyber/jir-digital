    Overview

    Roof Replacement in Anytown

    When a roof reaches the end of its service life, repeated repairs stop making financial sense. A full replacement lets us correct problems hidden beneath the surface — soft decking, poor ventilation, inadequate flashing — and start fresh with a complete roofing system.

    Every replacement includes removal of the existing roofing down to the deck, a decking inspection, replacement of damaged sheathing as needed, new underlayment and ice/water protection where appropriate, new flashing and drip edge, your chosen roofing material, and properly balanced ventilation.

    We help you compare materials, colors, and options in terms of appearance, durability, and budget — and we put the full scope in writing before any work begins.

    Common Problems

    Signs You May Need This Service

    • Roof nearing end of life

      Curling, cracking, and widespread granule loss signal the shingles are worn out.

    • Multiple or recurring leaks

      When leaks appear in several areas, the system has usually failed as a whole.

    • Sagging rooflines

      A dip in the roof can indicate moisture-damaged decking or structural issues.

    • Daylight in the attic

      Light coming through boards means gaps where water and pests can enter.
