    Overview

    Roof Leak Detection in Anytown

    Water can travel along rafters, decking, and insulation before it shows up indoors, which is why so many leak 'repairs' fail. Leak detection is a process of elimination: inspecting likely entry points from both the roof and the attic and following evidence like staining, rust, and damp insulation.

    We examine roof penetrations (vents, pipes, skylights, chimneys), step and wall flashing, valleys, ridge and hip caps, and areas where debris collects. Inside the attic, we look for water trails, mold, and nail-tip condensation that can mimic a leak.

    You receive photos and an explanation of the likely source, along with recommended repair options. Not every leak is a roof leak — sometimes the cause is condensation, a plumbing vent, or siding — and we'll tell you if that's what we find.

    Common Problems

    Signs You May Need This Service

    • Ceiling stains

      Brown rings or bubbling paint usually mean water has been entering for a while.

    • Leaks only in heavy rain

      Wind-driven rain often points to flashing or siding transition issues.

    • Attic condensation

      Poor ventilation can drip moisture that looks exactly like a leak.

    • Skylight & chimney leaks

      Penetrations are high-risk areas when flashing or seals age.
