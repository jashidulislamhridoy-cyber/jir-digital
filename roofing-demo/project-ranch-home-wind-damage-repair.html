    Ranch Home Wind Damage Repair

    Emergency protection and repair after high winds stripped shingles from the windward slope.

    The Challenge

    High winds removed a section of shingles and exposed underlayment on one slope.

    Our Solution

    We provided temporary protection, then replaced damaged shingles and underlayment on the affected slope and re-sealed lifted tabs nearby.

    The Result

    The affected slope was restored and the roof was returned to a weather-tight condition.
