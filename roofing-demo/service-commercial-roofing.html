    Common Problems

    Signs You May Need This Service

    • Ponding water

      Standing water accelerates membrane breakdown and adds structural load.

    • Seam & flashing failures

      Seams and perimeter flashing are common failure points on low-slope roofs.

    • Rooftop equipment damage

      HVAC curbs and foot traffic can puncture or wear the membrane.

    • Clogged drains & scuppers

      Blocked drainage leads to ponding, leaks, and overflow damage.
