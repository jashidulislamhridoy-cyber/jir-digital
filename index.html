Pricing questions

Good to know.

Is the ad budget included in the price?+

No. The package price is our management fee. Your ad budget is paid directly to Meta from your own ad account, so you always see exactly where it goes.

Can I change plans later?+

Yes. Many businesses start with Starter or Growth and move up once they see results. Plans are month-to-month, so you can upgrade or change before your next billing month.

Do you work with businesses outside the US?+

Yes — we work with businesses in the US, Canada, the UK and Australia. All prices are in US dollars.
